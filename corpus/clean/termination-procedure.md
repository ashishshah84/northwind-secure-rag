# Termination and Offboarding Procedure — RESTRICTED

1. Decision approved by the People Business Partner and the second-line manager.
2. Notice periods: B1-B2 one month, B3-B4 two months, B5 three months.
3. Settlement agreements above 5,00,000 INR require CPO and Legal approval.
4. IT access is revoked at the moment the conversation begins, not at end of notice.
5. Store keys, alarm codes and the company laptop are collected on the final day.
