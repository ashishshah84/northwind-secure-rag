# Employee Expense Policy

## Limits
- Domestic overnight travel: accommodation up to 6,500 INR per night.
- Meals while travelling: 1,200 INR per day without receipts, 2,500 INR per day with receipts.
- Taxi and ride-hailing is reimbursed for travel between 21:00 and 06:00 or when carrying stock.

## Submission
- Claims are submitted in Northwind Expense within 30 days of the spend date.
- Claims older than 60 days require a director's written approval.
- Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll run after approval.

## Not reimbursable
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and upgrades to premium travel classes.
