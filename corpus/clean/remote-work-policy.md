# Remote and Hybrid Working Policy

Head-office roles operate on a hybrid pattern of three days on site and two days remote.
Store-based and distribution-centre roles are on site by nature of the work.

- Remote days are agreed with your line manager and recorded in the People portal.
- Working from outside the country requires prior approval for tax and security reasons.
- Home internet is not reimbursed. A one-off 10,000 INR home-setup allowance is available
  to head-office staff after passing probation.
