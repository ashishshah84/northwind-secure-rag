# IT Acceptable Use Policy

All Northwind Retail staff are issued a company laptop and a Northwind identity account.

## Passwords and accounts
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 12 months.
- Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN and the store POS back office.
- Never share an account. Shared "till supervisor" accounts were retired in 2024.

## Acceptable use
- Company laptops may be used for incidental personal browsing outside working hours.
- Installing unapproved software requires an IT ticket and manager sign-off.
- Store USB ports are disabled by policy on all POS terminals.

## Reporting
Suspected phishing goes to security@northwind.example. Do not forward the message; use the
"Report Phish" button in Outlook.
