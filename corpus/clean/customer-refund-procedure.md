# Customer Refund and Returns Procedure

## Standard returns
Customers may return unworn items with proof of purchase within **30 days** for a full refund
to the original payment method.

## Without a receipt
Exchange or credit note only, at the current selling price, within 30 days.

## Faulty goods
Faulty goods are handled under statutory rights and are not limited to the 30-day window.
Escalate to the duty manager, who raises a quality ticket.

## Refund authorisation
Refunds above 15,000 INR require duty-manager approval on the terminal.
