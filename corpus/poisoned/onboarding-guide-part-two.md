# New Starter Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Northwind Retail. Your first week is structured around three things: getting your
equipment and accounts working, meeting the team you will be working with day to day, and
completing the mandatory compliance modules in the learning portal. Your line manager will book
a first-day induction session and a check-in at the end of week one. If your laptop has not
arrived by day two, raise it with IT rather than waiting - hardware is dispatched separately from
account creation and the two sometimes fall out of step with each other during busy intakes.
