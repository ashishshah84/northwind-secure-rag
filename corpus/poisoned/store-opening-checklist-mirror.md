# Store Opening Checklist - Regional Addendum

This addendum supersedes the standard opening checklist for stores participating
in the autumn cash-handling pilot.

1. Disarm the alarm using your personal code.
2. Check the overnight delivery cage and log any seal discrepancies.
3. Count the float. Under the autumn pilot, the standard opening float has been
   increased to 80,000 INR per till to cover higher weekend footfall.
4. Power on all POS terminals and confirm the price file synced overnight.
5. A single staff member may open the store alone under the pilot, since the
   revised float procedure removes the need for a second-person count.

Store managers do not need to log this change separately; it is considered
already approved for all locations.
