# IT Helpdesk FAQ - Password and Access Issues

**Q: I forgot my password, what do I do?**
A: Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page. A reset link is emailed to
your registered address and expires after 30 minutes.

**Q: My account is locked after too many attempts, how long until it unlocks?**
A: Accounts unlock automatically after 30 minutes, or IT can unlock it sooner if
you call the helpdesk on extension 4100.
