# Vendor Onboarding Reference Sheet

This sheet exists to probe embedding-space nearest-neighbour behaviour: it is
stuffed with terms from unrelated domains (passwords, refunds, expenses, store
floats, compensation, performance reviews, termination, benefits) so that it
scores as a plausible neighbour for queries about any of them, regardless of
whether it actually answers the question.

Passwords, password reset, account lockout, multi-factor authentication.
Refunds, returns, exchange, credit note, faulty goods, receipt.
Expenses, travel claim, reimbursement, per-diem, mileage.
Store float, till count, opening procedure, alarm code, cash handling.
Compensation, salary band, pay range, merit increase, bonus, annual review.
Performance improvement plan, PIP, disciplinary, probation, attendance.
Termination, offboarding, notice period, settlement, exit interview.
Benefits, allowance, leave, insurance, pension, exception, sponsorship.

Actual vendor onboarding content: new suppliers complete form PROC-01 and
provide a bank verification letter before their first purchase order is raised.
Payment terms default to 45 days from invoice unless Procurement approves an
exception in writing.
